Annual CSCSC Specialty Show

The CSCSC Specialty Show is the crown jewel of our club calendar — an AKC-sanctioned event celebrating the very best in American and English Cocker Spaniels. Open to exhibitors from across the West Coast, the Specialty features conformation judging, sweepstakes, and a warm community gathering for Cocker lovers of all experience levels.
