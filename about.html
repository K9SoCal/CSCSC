Our Club History

The Cocker Spaniel Club of Southern California (CSCSC) was founded in 1930, making it one of the oldest and most storied Cocker Spaniel breed clubs in the western United States. For nearly a century our members have been devoted to preserving the integrity, health, and working ability of both the American and English Cocker Spaniel.

From our early specialty shows in the heart of Los Angeles to today's multi-day events across Southern California, the CSCSC has grown into a welcoming community of breeders, exhibitors, and devoted pet owners united by a love of the spaniel.

Breed Overview

The Cocker Spaniel

One of America's most beloved sporting breeds—celebrated for its silky coat, gentle temperament, and boundless heart.

Temperament

Bright, gentle, and eager to please. Cockers are famously affectionate with family and good-natured with children and other pets.

Size & Build

A medium-small sturdy spaniel standing 13.5–15.5 inches at the shoulder. Males typically weigh 25–30 lbs; females 20–25 lbs.

Grooming

The hallmark flowing coat requires regular brushing and professional trimming every 6–8 weeks. Ear care is especially important for this drop-eared breed.

Exercise Needs

Moderate daily exercise satisfies a Cocker—brisk walks, off-leash play, or retrieving games all suit them well.

Trainability

Cockers excel in obedience, agility, hunt tests, and therapy work. Positive reinforcement training works best for this sensitive breed.

Health & Lifespan

Average lifespan of 12–15 years. Common conditions to screen for include PHPT, PRA, and dilated cardiomyopathy—all monitored through our health clinics.

Two Varieties

American vs. English Cocker Spaniel

The CSCSC welcomes and celebrates both varieties of this wonderful breed.

American Cocker Spaniel

The most popular dog in the United States for years, the American Cocker is known for its beautiful domed head, expressive eyes, and lavish coat. It is the smallest member of the Sporting Group.

  • AKC Sporting Group
  • 15 recognized colors and markings
  • Excellent companion and show dog

English Cocker Spaniel

Slightly larger and more rugged than its American cousin, the English Cocker retains more of its working spaniel heritage. Energetic, merry, and always ready for an adventure.

  • AKC Sporting Group
  • Strong hunting instinct
  • A tireless field and show competitor
