Commitment to Excellence

Health & Welfare Clinics

The CSCSC facilitates professional screenings and genetic testing to ensure the Cocker Spaniel remains healthy for generations to come.

Screening Services

What We Offer

Our clinics are open to all sporting breeds. Appointments are recommended; walk-ins are accommodated as scheduling allows.

OFA Eye Certification

Conducted by board-certified veterinary ophthalmologists to screen for cataracts, glaucoma, and PRA.

  • Annual screening recommended for all breeding stock
  • Results submitted directly to the OFA database
  • Open to all sporting breeds and mixed breeds

Cardiology Screening

Auscultation and echocardiograms provided by board-certified cardiologists to verify healthy heart function.

  • Advanced Doppler exams available on request
  • OFA certification paperwork included
  • Screens for dilated cardiomyopathy and valve disease

DNA & Genetic Testing

Cheek-swab collection for comprehensive genetic panel testing including PHPT, FN, and Exercise Induced Collapse.

  • Samples sent to accredited labs
  • Results within 3–4 weeks
  • Breeder counseling available

Orthopedic Evaluations

Hip and elbow radiographs reviewed by board-certified radiologists under the OFA protocol.

  • Preliminary readings available same day
  • Required by many top breeding programs
  • Open to all breeds 12 months and older

Common Questions

Clinic FAQ

Do I need to be a club member?

No. Our health clinics are open to all dog owners and breeders regardless of club membership.

How do I make an appointment?

Appointments are made through our contact form once a clinic date is announced.

What should I bring?

Bring your dog's vaccination records, AKC registration if applicable, and payment. Accepted forms of payment will be listed in the clinic announcement.

Are results shared with the OFA?

Eye and cardiac results can be submitted directly to the OFA registry on your behalf. You may request that results remain private.
